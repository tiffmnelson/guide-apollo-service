## Contact the Apollo Service Team

For all enquiries about the Australian Apollo Service, please contact us using the information below.

---

## Email Support

**Email**: [apollo-support@genome.edu.au](mailto:apollo-support@genome.edu.au)

**Response time**: We aim to respond to all enquiries within 1–2 business days.

### What to Include in Your Email

To help us assist you quickly, please include:

- **Your name and institution**
- **Your Apollo Instance name** (if applicable)
- **A clear description of your question or issue**
- **Any error messages** you have encountered
- **Steps you have already tried** to resolve the issue

---

## Contact Form

You can also use the contact form on the [Apollo Service Portal](https://apollo-portal.genome.edu.au/contact) to send us a message.

---

## Common Enquiries

### Application Status

If you have submitted an application for an Apollo Instance and would like to check the status, email [apollo-support@genome.edu.au](mailto:apollo-support@genome.edu.au) with your research group name.

### Technical Support

For technical issues with your Apollo Instance, email [apollo-support@genome.edu.au](mailto:apollo-support@genome.edu.au) with:
- Your instance name
- A description of the issue
- Screenshots (if applicable)

### Feature Requests

We welcome suggestions for new features or improvements. Email [apollo-support@genome.edu.au](mailto:apollo-support@genome.edu.au) with your ideas.

### Data Sharing and Collaboration

If you would like to discuss data sharing arrangements or collaborative projects, contact [apollo-support@genome.edu.au](mailto:apollo-support@genome.edu.au).
