## What is the Australian Apollo Service?

The Australian Apollo Service is a workbench for visualising, manipulating, analysing and interpreting life science data — specifically genome assemblies and genome annotations, and enabling the manual curation of genome annotations.

Apollo is a web-based tool for real-time collaborative curation and editing of genome annotations. The Australian Apollo Service provides hosted Apollo instances for Australian-based research groups and consortia, eliminating the need for local installation and system administration.

## Who Operates the Service?

The Australian Apollo Service is operated by:

- **Australian BioCommons** (hosted by The University of Melbourne) — overall service coordination
- **QCIF** (Queensland Cyber Infrastructure Foundation) — instance hosting and management
- **The University of Queensland** — technical support and development

## Funding and Support

The Australian Apollo Service is supported by:

- **Bioplatforms Australia** — funding and strategic direction
- **ARDC** (Australian Research Data Commons) — infrastructure support
- **AARNet** — network infrastructure
- **Pawsey Supercomputing Research Centre** — computational resources
- **NCRIS** (National Collaborative Research Infrastructure Strategy) — core funding

## How to Cite

When papers acknowledge the Australian Apollo Service, they support continued research infrastructure investments by institutions and governments.

When you use the Australian Apollo Service to support your publication or project, please cite its use with the following acknowledgement statement and web address:

> This work was supported by the Australian Apollo Service https://apollo-portal.genome.edu.au/ provided by Australian BioCommons and Operational Partners.
